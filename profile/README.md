## NextBlock

A video social app without ads or an algorithm. Built on Bitcoin and Nostr.

I'm Ian. I build the thing. My wife Mandana asks the questions and co-founded the company
with me. I'm the one who kept trying to teach her Bitcoin until her attention span ran out.
She started the Flirting with Bitcoin podcast; I was the guy at the whiteboard.

When we tried to grow the show, we hit the wall everyone hits: the only way to reach people
was to rent attention from big tech and fund the very surveillance machine we were explaining
our way out of.

That felt wrong. So I built the alternative.

NextBlock is a city. You claim a block, your own corner of it, and your attention is yours
there. No ads you didn't choose. No algorithm deciding what you see. No one harvesting you.

### The ATTN protocol

I came up with the attention protocol: the rules for people to own and price their own
attention, instead of a platform pricing it for them. Billboard is the first implementation.
We publish the spec when billboard launches; until then the code stays private.

### The book

Mandana wrote [The Attention Protocol](https://theattentionprotocol.com): a modern love story
about the most expensive thing we keep giving away for free. Read it free in the city's library.
