**Advertisers & Creators**

## The Solution

But, with new technology, comes new opportunities. Powered by Bitcoin and Nostr, NextBlock is the first and only social media company where people set the price of their attention to view advertisements through an opt-in, decentralized Attention Marketplace. On NextBlock, the C.H.A.O.S. Cartel can't harvest your attention against your will. You are not a user to be sold. You are a citizen with rights.

Only an opt-in, decentralized Attention Marketplace can free us from exploitation. To prevent abuse, this Marketplace must be made available as a public good, have mutually beneficial incentives for buyers and sellers, and it must fund itself. We don't need a government to make and maintain this public good. Nor do we need a government to regulate this because it is a consensual marketplace. NextBlock will build it, test it, and make it available to the public.

With transparent marketplace mechanics, buyers - the advertisers - and the sellers - citizens - are matched to complete a transaction at an agreed upon price. The marketplace also directs the buyer to pay for each facilitating technology contributing to the ad experience. Everyone remains incentivized to devote resources to improve each part of the Attention Marketplace.

The incentive structure for this public good dismantles and prevents future exploitation and monopolies. NextBlock will be the first, but not last, to allow people to control the monetization of their attention.

## Our Principles

- **Identity Ownership**: On NextBlock, we don't own your identity, you do. Leveraging the Nostr Protocol, each citizen controls their own digital identity, social network, and where their data is stored. Nextblock, a Nostr app, is simply a browser for your social media.

- **Payment Control**: On NextBlock, we don't decide who gets paid, you do. Leveraging the Bitcoin Protocol, citizens who earn money selling their attention can pay creators directly when they enjoy their content.

- **Ad Timing**: And on NextBlock, we don't decide when the ads are shown, Bitcoin does. Ads will only interrupt your scrolling when a new Bitcoin block is mined, every 10 minutes or so. Get it? Next - Block!
